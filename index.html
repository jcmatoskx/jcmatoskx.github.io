About Porto Climbing

Porto Climbing is an Association dedicated to making the world of outdoor rock climbing accessible, safe, and truly rewarding for everyone in the Porto region and surrounding areas in the north of Portugal.

Our certified instructors combine years of real rock experience with a love for teaching, ensuring you learn at your pace, with total safety and maximum fun. We provide all technical equipment and support, so you can focus on learning, connecting, and enjoying the moment.

Based in the Porto region, Portugal, we run regular outdoor climbing sessions, guided rock climbing tours, intensive courses, and inspiring climbing trips to Portugal's best crags as well as other world-class destinations. For us, climbing is not just about reaching the top, it's about personal growth, trust, fun and the friendships we build along the way.

Rock Climbing FAQs

Common Questions and Answers

What types of climbing activities do you offer?

While we focus on the outdoor, we offer indoor and outdoor rock climbing for all skill levels, including bouldering, lead climbing, top-rope climbing, multi-pitch and more.

Do I need prior experience to participate?

No experience is necessary. Our instructors provide guidance and safety briefings for beginners.

What equipment is provided, and what should I bring?

We provide all necessary safety climbing gear. If you have your own please feel free to bring it. If you have your own climbing shoes that's ideal — if you don't, please let us know in advance. Please wear comfortable sports clothing, and bring water and snacks.

What is the schedule for the climbing activities and courses?

Contact us and we will happily provide you with information on all the latest events and the most up-to-date schedule :)
